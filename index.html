IDiagnosisAlaa Haider

A healthy company running an unhealthy process.

Thakhr Health Insurance is a mid-to-large UAE insurer covering outpatient, inpatient, pharmacy and dental care. Its people are careful. The delay comes from a process where a human still has to read every single claim.

ذَخْـر
The name is the brief

Thakhr means something precious, stored and protected for the day it is needed. Health cover stops being that when a claim sits in a queue for two weeks.

Three root inefficiencies

Manual handling, data fragmentation, analytical blindness.

The insurer holds rich data and makes almost no predictions from it. Kraus et al., 2021

The person missing from the diagram

The member learns of a rejection at the pharmacy counter.

Then she calls to ask why. She sees no status and no reason. Every design decision that follows starts from that counter.

IIThe answerReem Aljneibi

Every inefficiency is an opportunity, read from the other side.

Our design position is preservative. We add an intelligent layer on top of what the UAE has already built, and we replace nothing that works.

Manual claim handling

Automated reading and rulesModern AI reads semi-structured documents, applies rules and flags variance. Davenport & Kalakota, 2019 · Secinaro et al., 2021

Data fragmentation

One member-and-insurance layerA single layer binds the existing systems. Each party sees only what its role permits.

Analytical blindness

Predictive population modellingCatch diabetic, cardiac and hypertension risk early, and act before it becomes a claim.

The one decision we refused to fully automate.

Clear & low-risk

Settled automatically, in minutes.

The routine majority. The member watches the claim clear in the portal.

Grey area or high value

Escalated to a person.

The adjuster opens a prepared assessment, not an empty file, so even escalated claims move faster.

Human-in-the-loop from day one. It answers the ethical constraint directly, and it is what makes the agent acceptable to a regulator. Jiang et al., 2017 · Reddy et al., 2019

A critique of our own artifact

Single

True only while it is the only list. The real risk is stakeholders keeping private spreadsheets on the side.

Prioritised

Never better than its estimates, and our WSJF scoring is subjective and uncalibrated.

Dynamic

Reordered too often, it becomes unstable. Responsiveness and thrash look identical from outside.

The artifact does not solve these problems. It makes them visible, which is a more modest claim than the literature makes, and one we can defend.

VPeopleHessa Eissa

A nine-person Scrum Team, and a hybrid way to release.

Pure Scrum cannot release freely inside an insurer. So we set Agile delivery inside the frame the industry requires.

1×

Product Owner

The Operations Director. Orders the backlog by member outcomes, not by technical interest.

1×

Scrum Master

A servant leader. In an insurer, the impediments are approval lines, not code.

7×

Developers

Three engineers, a data scientist, an AI specialist, a UX designer, and an embedded claims expert who turns rules into requirements.

This is Water-Scrum-Fall, the balance of agility and discipline. Agility within the constraints, not against them. West, 2011 · Boehm & Turner, 2004

Trust and learning

On a project that automates decisions, psychological safety is not a soft topic.

Trust is built by practice. The Daily Scrum does most of the work, because blockers that are visible cannot fester. Denning, 2018 · Layton et al., 2020

The change we will not dress up

Adjusters will fear automation.

Leadership must say it plainly: Dakhtar removes routine work, not jobs. That promise is backed by retraining toward investigation.

Conflict · a four-step framework
  1. i.

    Define the problem

    Separate the problem from the people.

  2. ii.

    Hear everyone

    Listen actively before deciding.

  3. iii.

    Find common ground

    Usually a shared wish to ship this Sprint.

  4. iv.

    Solve it together

    Win–win, with the Scrum Master mediating.

When facilitation runs out, the Product Owner decides priority and the developers decide implementation. The people doing the work own the plan.

VIIConclusionsHessa Eissa

What this project found.

Finding 01

The problem is concentrated.

It sits at the manual interface between provider and insurer, the source of the 50.4-hour turnaround.

Finding 02

AI is the right technology.

Adjudication is language and judgement work that AI can now do under human oversight.

Finding 03 · least intuitive

Delivery matters as much as technology.

Not pure Agile, but a hybrid with compliance gates. Rigby et al., 2016

Reading the KPI cards correctly

The yearly averages look like a miss because they include the manual months. December is the real steady state. The gap between the two measures the change itself.
